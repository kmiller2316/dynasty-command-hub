📖 What do these labels mean? (legend)
Win-Now Index 0–100 — how positioned a team is to win now vs. build for later. Blends roster strength (value rank), how much value sits in 27+ vs. under-25 players, future draft-pick stockpiling, and record once games are played. High = win-now, low = rebuild.
Overall profile (from Win-Now Index): All-In Contender ≥65 · Balanced Competitor 45–64 · Retooling 25–44 · Full Rebuild <25
Age profile (value-weighted avg age): Youth-Focused <24.5 · Prime Window 24.5–26.5 · Win-Now 26.5–28 · Aging Core 28+
Positional bias: X-Heavy = well above league-average share of value at that position · X-Needy = notably thin there · Balanced = no strong tilt
Pick capital: Pick Rich = >30% above league-avg pick value · Average · Pick Poor = >30% below
Roster construction: Star-Driven = top-3 players carry most value · Balanced Depth = value spread out · No Stars = no elite piece
Trade posture: Pick Accumulator = holds surplus future picks · Win-Now Buyer = has spent picks for players · Passive = near league-average pick capital
Matchup luck (record vs. what their scoring rank predicts): Lucky winning more than points suggest · Even · Unlucky losing despite good scoring. Shows “Even” for everyone before the season starts.
